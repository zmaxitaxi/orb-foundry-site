Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026.

Orb Foundry is a personal project used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube access

The app requests YouTube upload and read-only access solely to upload videos and check for duplicate uploads on that channel.

Personal data

  • The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from anyone else.
  • OAuth credentials are kept privately by the owner and used only for this purpose.

Google APIs

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked anytime from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner through the Orb Foundry YouTube channel.
